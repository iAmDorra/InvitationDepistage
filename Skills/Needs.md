Calculate the invitation date based on eligibility for a screening type, a date of birth, and today's date.
The invitation date is calculated by adding the day of birth, the month of birth, the number of months associated with the screening type, and the current year.
Verify that the invitation date is valid; if not, adjust it to the next valid working day. 
If the invitation date is earlier than today's date, add one year to the current year. 
If the invitation date falls on a public holiday, adjust it to the next working day.
If the invitation date falls on a Saturday or Sunday, adjust it to the following Monday.

Today : 01/01/2023

So the invitation date is 15/05/2023
